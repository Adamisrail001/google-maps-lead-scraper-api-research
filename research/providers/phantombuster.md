# PhantomBuster — Verification Record

**Verified:** 2026-09-23 — partially. phantombuster.com renders via JavaScript (fetch returned no content); facts below come from PhantomBuster's own support/blog pages and third-party reviews surfaced in search, marked accordingly.

- **Google Maps product:** yes — "Google Maps Search Export" and "Google Maps Search to Contact Data" Phantoms (their own support articles: support.phantombuster.com/hc/en-us/articles/26971122390674 and 26971113168146). The Contact Data phantom visits business websites and extracts selected contact details, with an email-enrichment/verification step.
- **Real API:** PhantomBuster has a developer API for launching phantoms programmatically, but this was **unverified in this research** (docs not fetched). The product is an automation platform first, not a data API.
- **Auth / self-serve:** 14-day free trial (2h execution time, 5 phantom slots, 50 email credits, no card) per third-party review.
- **Pricing (third-party, 2026):** Starter $69/mo, Pro $159/mo, Team $439/mo — billed by **execution time** (minutes of phantom runtime), not per record. Cost per 1K records therefore workload-dependent and not directly computable in advance (relevant to elimination criterion E4).
- **Lead fields:** business name, address, phone, reviews + contact data (emails, verified) via the enrichment step.
- **Intents served:** lead generation (explicitly), contact enrichment, automation. Scale posture unclear — execution-time budgets cap throughput.
- **Category: ADJACENT** — lead-gen capable with email enrichment, but an automation platform with execution-time pricing rather than a Google Maps data API; API-first usage unverified.
